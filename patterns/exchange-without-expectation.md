# Exchange Without Expectation

This interface allows exchange
without promising outcome.

No delivery is guaranteed.
No benefit is described.
No future state is implied.

---

## Principle

Expectation distorts exchange.
This field removes expectation.

Money may pass.
Nothing follows.

---

## Interface Constraints

- No product description.
- No feature list.
- No roadmap.
- No updates promised.

The exchange stands alone,
without narrative continuation.

---

## Economic Integrity

If payment occurs:
- It is accepted without celebration.
- It is recorded without emphasis.
- It does not modify the system.

If no payment occurs:
- Nothing changes.

---

## Closure

Exchange completes itself.
No memory is required.
No obligation is formed.
