# Exit Without Collapse

The system allows departure
without consequence.

Exit is not failure.
Exit is not abandonment.

---

## Principle

A valid system must survive its creator’s silence.

Dependency is a structural flaw.
Attachment is a hidden liability.

---

## Exit Conditions

- No shutdown required
- No announcement expected
- No cleanup ritual

---

## What Remains

- Structure
- Records
- Availability

---

## What Leaves

- Intent
- Attention
- Explanation

---

## Closure

Nothing breaks.
Nothing ends.
The system simply stands.
