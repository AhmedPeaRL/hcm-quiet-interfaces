# Persistence Without Scaling

The system continues
without expansion.

Persistence is not growth.
Stability is not stagnation.

---

## Principle

Scaling introduces noise.
Noise demands control.
Control corrupts presence.

This field rejects that sequence.

---

## Allowed State

- To exist
- To remain reachable
- To stay intact

---

## Forbidden Transitions

- Viral loops
- Growth targets
- Expansion strategies
- “Next phase” narratives

---

## Integrity Clause

Continuation does not require justification.
Duration does not imply ambition.
Silence is not failure.

---

## Closure

The system stays.
Nothing is added.
Nothing is chased.
