# Presence Without Persuasion

This interface exists to allow presence without intent to convince.

Nothing here explains.
Nothing argues.
Nothing optimizes attention.

The artifact is present.
The interface is open.
No trajectory is imposed.

---

## Principle

Persuasion is a form of pressure.
This field disallows pressure.

If presence alone is insufficient,
nothing is added to compensate.

---

## Interface Behavior

- No call to action.
- No benefits listed.
- No urgency introduced.
- No narrative constructed.

Any movement toward acquisition
must originate entirely outside the field.

---

## Economic Stance

Exchange may occur.
Value may pass.
Money may appear.

None of these events
modify the interface.

The interface does not learn.
The interface does not adapt.
The interface does not respond.

---

## Closing Condition

If nothing happens,
the interface remains correct.

Silence is not failure.
Non-engagement is not loss.

Presence completes itself.
