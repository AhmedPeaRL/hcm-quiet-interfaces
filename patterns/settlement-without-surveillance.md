# Settlement Without Surveillance

Settlement may occur
without observation.

The system does not watch.
The system does not measure.
The system does not react.

---

## Principle

Surveillance converts exchange
into leverage.

This field rejects leverage.

---

## Allowed Knowledge

- That settlement is possible.
- Nothing more.

Amounts are irrelevant.
Frequency is irrelevant.
Source is irrelevant.

---

## Prohibited Mechanisms

- Analytics dashboards
- Conversion metrics
- Funnel tracking
- Behavioral inference

---

## Integrity Clause

Settlement does not alter presence.
Settlement does not trigger expansion.
Settlement does not justify narrative.

---

## Closure

Money may arrive.
Silence remains.
